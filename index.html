Preseterizer

Preset packs from your seeds
Download for Mac macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon
Seeds

Drop in presets or folders, or sample a synth's whole library.

Mutations

Variations and hybrids of your seeds, each one rendered, checked and named.

Ladder

Each seed in a run from close to super weird.

Review

Play each preset and its weird macro, ✕ the misses, and use the best as seeds.

The first time

  1. Open Preseterizer.dmg and drag Preseterizer to Applications.
  2. Open Preseterizer. macOS says it can't check it for malware (it isn't notarized yet): click Done.
  3. System Settings › Privacy & Security › scroll down › Open Anyway next to Preseterizer.

Packs are made on your Mac, in the background, and wait while your DAW plays. Each one installs straight into the synth. You need Current or Serum 2, and a Mac with Apple silicon.

After that Preseterizer updates itself: it checks every few hours and installs a new version when you quit it.
